About the masjid

One of Birmingham’s most active mosques

The Muath Trust opened the Amanah Masjid on Henley Street in 2006, a short walk from the Trust’s home at the Bordesley Centre. The Trust was founded in 1990 by members of the Yemeni community, the oldest Muslim community to settle in Birmingham.

Today the masjid serves brothers and sisters from across Sparkbrook and beyond, with daily prayers, Qur’an schools for children and a weekly programme of classes.

  • Brothers’ prayer hallFive daily prayers and Jumu‘ah
  • Sisters’ prayer hallA dedicated space for sisters
  • Qur’an schoolsDaily and weekend Tahfeeth for boys and girls
  • Study circlesTafsir, Seerah, Qur’an and Tajweed, in Arabic

Qur’an schools and weekly classes

Our Tahfeeth teachers are fully qualified and most are huffaath. Children learn to recite with Tajweed, study the Qur’anic sciences and memorise the Qur’an.
